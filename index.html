Harrow Bay Clinic

A medical mystery and clinic tycoon game

The game

Take over your grandfather's old clinic in a foggy seaside town. Ask the right questions, examine your patients, run tests and work out what's wrong, then grow the clinic into the finest hospital on the coast while you uncover the Bay's oldest mystery.

This is a game, not medical advice. Illnesses are simplified and every medicine name is made up.
